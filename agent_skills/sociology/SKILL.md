# SKILL: Sociology & Collective Action
`agent_skills/sociology/SKILL.md`

## 1. Purpose & Domain Scope
Guides the modeling of collective action, institutional trust, social stratification, informal community solidarity, gossip networks, and public sphere deliberations.

## 2. When to Use
- Implementing public deliberations, town hall meetings (*asambleas comunitarias*), and collective petitions.
- Modeling social cohesion, polarization, in-group vs out-group dynamics, and community fission.
- Simulating social capital, mutual assistance (*compadrazgo*), and civic mobilization.

## 3. Workflows & Procedures
1. **Network Topology**: Track interpersonal ties (kinship, neighbor, coworker, customer).
2. **Collective Action Thresholds**: Implement Granovetter-style threshold models for civic participation and protest.
3. **Public Deliberation**: Structure assembly voting and consensus algorithms respecting Ostrom CPR design principles.

## 4. Technical References
- Granovetter, M. (1978). *Threshold Models of Collective Behavior*.
- Coleman, J. S. (1990). *Foundations of Social Theory*.
- Ostrom, E. (1990). *Governing the Commons*.

## 5. Validation Criteria
- Fission occurs under high internal variance and unresolvable conflict.
- Mutual aid buffers households against external climatic and economic shocks.
