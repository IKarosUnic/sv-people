# SKILL: Artificial Life & Autonomous Emergence
`agent_skills/artificial_life/SKILL.md`

## 1. Purpose & Domain Scope
Guides the implementation of open-ended, autonomous agent behaviors, metabolic loops, spontaneous community formation, and self-organizing social structures without script rails or per-agent LLM invocations.

## 2. When to Use
- Designing metabolic loops (hunger, fatigue, thermal comfort).
- Implementing emergent coordination, spontaneous market stalls, or informal neighborhood watches.
- Ensuring autonomous agents survive and develop dynamic cultures in a local-first environment.

## 3. Workflows & Procedures
1. **Utility Formulation**: Define bounded rational utility functions based on physiological needs, social belonging, and financial security.
2. **Local Interaction Rules**: Agents interact with proximate neighbors and local physical infrastructure.
3. **Emergence Verification**: Measure whether macro-phenomena (e.g. market price clearing, cultural divergence) emerge from micro-actions without top-down decree.

## 4. Technical References
- Langton, C. G. (1989). *Artificial Life*.
- Epstein, J. M., & Axtell, R. (1996). *Growing Artificial Societies: Social Science from the Bottom Up*.

## 5. Validation Criteria
- Zero mandatory LLM dependency for baseline life loop.
- Emergent behaviors pass statistical sanity tests (e.g. non-zero variance, absence of infinite deadlocks).
