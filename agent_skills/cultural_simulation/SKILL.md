# SKILL: Cultural Simulation & Entertainment Dynamics
`agent_skills/cultural_simulation/SKILL.md`

## 1. Purpose & Domain Scope
Guides the simulation of cultural evolution, music preferences, generational taste shifts, sports fandom, local sports tournaments, and community entertainment rituals.

## 2. When to Use
- Implementing music preferences, musical genres (cumbia, salsa, rock en español, reggaeton), and generational dynamics.
- Simulating sports ecosystems (football leagues, municipal derbies, match outcomes, fan rivalry).
- Modeling cultural festivals, communal celebrations, and heritage transmission.

## 3. Workflows & Procedures
1. **Taste Vector Formulation**: Model agent music and sports preferences as Dirichlet/normalized vectors influenced by age, parental taste, and peer network.
2. **Entertainment Events**: Trigger sports matches and music releases with procedural metadata (never copyrighted lyrics or audio streams).
3. **Social Resonance**: Match results and viral songs spread through social networks, boosting communal mood or sparking debate.
4. **Conservation & Solvency**: Stadium tickets, festival food, and music venues must observe strict accounting conservation.

## 4. Technical References
- *Cultural Transmission and Evolution* (Cavalli-Sforza & Feldman, 1981).
- *Axelrod's Dissemination of Culture Model* (Axelrod, 1997).
- Salvadoran sports history: *Primera División de El Salvador* (FAS, Alianza, Águila, Firpo) and local canton football.

## 5. Validation Criteria
- Zero copyright infringement (procedural fictional titles, composers, teams).
- Probabilistic cultural adoption; older generations show resistance to emergent trends.
- Fandom and music discussions generate authentic social dialogue without forcing 100% of the population into conformity.
